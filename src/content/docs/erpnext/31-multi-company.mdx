ERPNext gives both shapes of the classic "one DB with tenant_id vs database-per-tenant" decision: **multi-company** in one site, **multi-site** with separate databases, or cost centers. Choose by isolation, reporting, and ownership needs.

## Multi-company in one site

Multiple companies in a single site: each gets its own chart of accounts, warehouses, cost centers, and financials, while sharing the Item master, User pool, and DocTypes.

### Company hierarchy

A company with `is_group=1` is a non-postable parent; children point at it via `parent_company`. Creating a child auto-generates its Chart of Accounts, default warehouses, cost centers, and payable/receivable/stock accounts:

```python
# Parent company
parent = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Company",
    "company_name": "ScoopJoy Group",
    "abbr": "SJG",
    "country": "India",
    "default_currency": "INR",
    "is_group": 1,
    "chart_of_accounts": "Standard"
})
parent.insert()

# Child companies per outlet
outlets = [
    {"name": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North", "abbr": "SJN"},
    {"name": "ScoopJoy - Outlet South", "abbr": "SJS"},
    {"name": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen", "abbr": "SJC"},
]

for outlet in outlets:
    child = frappe.get_doc({
        "doctype": "Company",
        "company_name": outlet["name"],
        "abbr": outlet["abbr"],
        "country": "India",
        "default_currency": "INR",
        "parent_company": "ScoopJoy Group",
        "chart_of_accounts": "Standard"
    })
    child.insert()
```

:::tip[Consistent chart of accounts]
Use the "Standard" template for every child company — it keeps consolidated reporting simple.
:::

### Per-company default accounts

Account names carry the company abbr suffix (`- SJN`) — that's how one database namespaces accounts across companies:

```python
company = frappe.get_doc("Company", "ScoopJoy - Outlet North")
company.default_bank_account = "HDFC Bank - SJN"
company.default_cash_account = "Cash - SJN"
company.default_receivable_account = "Debtors - SJN"
company.default_payable_account = "Creditors - SJN"
company.default_expense_account = "Cost of Goods Sold - SJN"
company.default_income_account = "Sales - SJN"
company.cost_center = "Main - SJN"
company.round_off_account = "Round Off - SJN"
company.write_off_account = "Write Off - SJN"
company.save()
```

### Shared Item master, per-company stock

Items are global; stock is per warehouse, and warehouses belong to a company:

```python
warehouse_north = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Warehouse",
    "warehouse_name": "Finished Goods",
    "company": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North",
    "parent_warehouse": "All Warehouses - SJN"
})
warehouse_north.insert()

# Same item, different warehouses per company
stock_north = frappe.db.get_value("Bin",
    {"item_code": "VANILLA-100ML", "warehouse": "Finished Goods - SJN"}, "actual_qty") or 0
stock_south = frappe.db.get_value("Bin",
    {"item_code": "VANILLA-100ML", "warehouse": "Finished Goods - SJS"}, "actual_qty") or 0
```

## Inter-company transactions

Central Kitchen → Outlet transfers flow as: declare allowed counterparties, create an internal customer/supplier pair, then post matching documents on both sides.

### Setup: allowed counterparties + internal parties

Mark accounts as "Inter Company Account" in each chart, add `allowed_to_transact_with` on both Company records, then create the internal customer/supplier (`is_internal_customer`/`is_internal_supplier` + `represents_company`):

```python
company_north = frappe.get_doc("Company", "ScoopJoy - Outlet North")
company_north.append("allowed_to_transact_with", {"company": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen"})
company_north.save()

company_kitchen = frappe.get_doc("Company", "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen")
company_kitchen.append("allowed_to_transact_with", {"company": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North"})
company_kitchen.save()

# Internal customer: Central Kitchen bills Outlet North
internal_customer = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Customer",
    "customer_name": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North (Internal)",
    "customer_type": "Company",
    "customer_group": "Internal",
    "territory": "India",
    "is_internal_customer": 1,
    "represents_company": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North",
    "companies": [{"company": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen"}]
})
internal_customer.insert()

# Internal supplier: Outlet North receives from Central Kitchen
internal_supplier = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Supplier",
    "supplier_name": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen (Internal)",
    "supplier_type": "Company",
    "supplier_group": "Internal",
    "is_internal_supplier": 1,
    "represents_company": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen",
    "companies": [{"company": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North"}]
})
internal_supplier.insert()
```

### Inter-company invoice

```python
# Central Kitchen issues a Sales Invoice
si = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Sales Invoice",
    "company": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen",
    "customer": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North (Internal)",
    "is_internal_customer": 1,
    "update_stock": 1,
    "set_warehouse": "Finished Goods - SJC",
    "items": [{"item_code": "VANILLA-100ML", "qty": 500, "rate": 40}]
})
si.insert()
si.submit()

# Generate the matching Purchase Invoice in Outlet North's books
from erpnext.accounts.doctype.sales_invoice.sales_invoice import (
    make_inter_company_purchase_invoice
)
pi = make_inter_company_purchase_invoice(si.name)
pi.set_warehouse = "Finished Goods - SJN"
pi.insert()
pi.submit()
```

### Inter-company journal entry (royalty / fees)

Non-stock transactions mirror into the counterparty's books with reversed debit/credit:

```python
# Outlet North pays a management fee to ScoopJoy Group
jv = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Journal Entry",
    "voucher_type": "Inter Company Journal Entry",
    "company": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North",
    "accounts": [
        {
            "account": "Management Fee Expense - SJN",
            "debit_in_account_currency": 50000,
            "cost_center": "Main - SJN"
        },
        {
            "account": "Inter Company Payable - SJN",
            "credit_in_account_currency": 50000,
            "party_type": "Supplier",
            "party": "ScoopJoy Group (Internal)"
        }
    ]
})
jv.insert()
jv.submit()
```

### Inter-company stock transfer

Delivery Note on one side, linked Purchase Receipt on the other — moves stock between warehouses:

```python
# Step 1: Central Kitchen ships
dn = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Delivery Note",
    "company": "ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen",
    "customer": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North (Internal)",
    "is_internal_customer": 1,
    "set_warehouse": "Finished Goods - SJC",
    "items": [{
        "item_code": "VANILLA-100ML",
        "qty": 200,
        "rate": 40,
        "target_warehouse": "Finished Goods - SJN"
    }]
})
dn.insert()
dn.submit()

# Step 2: Outlet North receives into its own warehouse (auto-linked Purchase Receipt)
```

### Consolidated financial statements

**Accounting > Consolidated Financial Statement**, or in code:

```python
from erpnext.accounts.report.consolidated_financial_statement.consolidated_financial_statement import execute

filters = frappe._dict({
    "company": "ScoopJoy Group",
    "filter_based_on": "Fiscal Year",
    "period_start_date": "2025-04-01",
    "period_end_date": "2026-03-31",
    "periodicity": "Yearly",
    "report": "Profit and Loss Statement"
})

columns, data, message, chart = execute(filters)
```

:::caution[Intercompany elimination is not fully automated in v16]
Automatic elimination entries during consolidation aren't complete — create manual elimination journal entries for intercompany revenue/expense first. Track GitHub issue #48351.
:::

### User permissions per outlet

Scope a franchise manager to one company with a User Permission (`allow="Company"`, `apply_to_all_doctypes=1` applies across all company-linked DocTypes), plus a Role Profile for granular access:

```python
frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "User Permission",
    "user": "manager.north@scoopjoy.com",
    "allow": "Company",
    "for_value": "ScoopJoy - Outlet North",
    "apply_to_all_doctypes": 1
}).insert()

role_profile = frappe.get_doc({
    "doctype": "Role Profile",
    "role_profile": "Franchise Manager",
    "roles": [
        {"role": "Sales User"},
        {"role": "Stock User"},
        {"role": "Accounts User"},
        {"role": "POS User"}
    ]
})
role_profile.insert()

user = frappe.get_doc("User", "manager.north@scoopjoy.com")
user.role_profile_name = "Franchise Manager"
user.save()
```

See [Chapter 9](/erpnext/09-permissions-security/) for the full permission model.

## Multi-site (multi-tenant): one bench, isolated sites

Each site has its own database, files, and config, sharing the codebase — the strongest isolation short of separate servers. Nginx routes by `Host` header:

<Mermaid title="Nginx routes by Host header into one bench's isolated sites" height={320} code={`
flowchart TB
  Nginx["Nginx<br/>routes by Host header"] --> A["north.scoopjoy.com<br/>own DB · own files"]
  Nginx --> B["south.scoopjoy.com<br/>own DB · own files"]
  Nginx --> C["central.scoopjoy.com<br/>own DB · own files"]
  subgraph bench["Single bench (shared code)"]
    A
    B
    C
  end
`} />

```bash
bench config dns_multitenant on

bench new-site north.scoopjoy.com --mariadb-root-password secret --admin-password admin123
bench new-site south.scoopjoy.com --mariadb-root-password secret --admin-password admin123

bench --site north.scoopjoy.com install-app erpnext
bench --site south.scoopjoy.com install-app erpnext
bench --site north.scoopjoy.com install-app scoopjoy
bench --site south.scoopjoy.com install-app scoopjoy

bench setup nginx
sudo systemctl reload nginx
```

Site management — `bench --site all` fans an operation across every tenant:

```bash
bench --site all list-apps
bench --site north.scoopjoy.com backup --with-files
bench --site north.scoopjoy.com migrate
bench --site all migrate
bench drop-site old-franchise.scoopjoy.com --force
bench --site north.scoopjoy.com console
```

## Multi-company vs multi-site

| Criteria | Multi-company (one site) | Multi-site (multi-tenant) |
|---|---|---|
| Data isolation | Shared DB, permission-based | Fully separate databases |
| Shared masters | Items, Users, DocTypes | Nothing shared |
| Consolidated reports | Built-in | External aggregation needed |
| User management | One pool, per-company permissions | Separate users per site |
| Customization | Same across companies | Independent per site |
| Performance | Single DB bottleneck at scale | Each site scales independently |
| Maintenance | One site to back up/update | N sites to maintain |
| Inter-company txns | Native | API integration required |

## Franchise decision matrix

### Option A — one company, cost centers per outlet

<Mermaid title="Option A: single company, one cost center per outlet" height={260} code={`
flowchart TB
  SJ["ScoopJoy<br/>(single company)"] --> CN["Cost Center: Outlet North"]
  SJ --> CS["Cost Center: Outlet South"]
  SJ --> CK["Cost Center: Central Kitchen"]
  SJ --> CH["Cost Center: Head Office"]
`} />

Simple, one chart of accounts, easy reporting — but no true financial isolation or per-outlet balance sheets. Best for 2–5 outlets under single ownership.

### Option B — parent + child companies (recommended)

<Mermaid title="Option B: parent group + child company per outlet" height={260} code={`
flowchart TB
  G["ScoopJoy Group<br/>parent · is_group=1"] --> N["ScoopJoy - Outlet North"]
  G --> S["ScoopJoy - Outlet South"]
  G --> C["ScoopJoy - Central Kitchen"]
  G --> H["ScoopJoy - Head Office"]
`} />

True per-outlet financials, native consolidated reporting, native inter-company transactions — at the cost of more setup and user-permission discipline. Best for 5–50 outlets under one ownership group.

### Option C — separate sites per franchise partner

<Mermaid title="Option C: separate site (own DB) per franchise partner" height={280} code={`
flowchart TB
  Bench["Bench"] --> A["north.scoopjoy.com<br/>Franchise Partner A"]
  Bench --> B["south.scoopjoy.com<br/>Franchise Partner B"]
  Bench --> C["central.scoopjoy.com<br/>Company-owned"]
  Bench --> H["hq.scoopjoy.com<br/>Head Office"]
`} />

Maximum isolation and independent customization — but no built-in consolidation and inter-site sync needs APIs. Best for 50+ outlets or independently-owned franchisees.

| Factor | A: Cost centers | B: Child companies | C: Separate sites |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Setup complexity | Low | Medium | High |
| Financial isolation | None | Per company | Complete |
| Consolidated reports | Native | Native | Custom |
| Inter-outlet stock | Simple transfer | Inter-company txn | API-based |
| Per-outlet P&L | Cost-center filter | Native per company | Native per site |
| Franchisee independence | None | Limited | Full |
| Scalability | Limited | Good | Best |
| Recommended outlets | 2–5 | 5–50 | 50+ / independent |
